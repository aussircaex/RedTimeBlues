
History in *Cyberpunk* diverges from our own largely in the 1980s.

By 1994, over 21 million Americans were homeless. While business districts were clean, well-lit showcases of technology and innovation, they were ringed by "Combat Zones" of squalid suburbs and slums.

The US Government devolved into mob rule, where the ruling clique conspired to plunder the treasury and resources of the country for their own personal benefit *(this is alternate history, I promise)*. Social Security, Medicare, and all welfare programs were defunded or looted, and only the military was lavished with funding to enable war-based imperialism. With Congress complacent or incapable of stopping them, democracy essentially came to an end.

A number of individual states broke away from Washington, including California (which then split into North and South), Texas, Oregon, Washington, and both Dakotas, becoming known as the "Free States". This only accelerated the collapse of the nation as a whole.

Encroaching climate change led to years of drought, causing mass abandonment of local farms and businesses in the Midwest. Giant Agricorporations bought up the properties, and an unchecked cycle of offshoring and corporate raiding led to a general economic collapse, as millions became homeless and were forced to travel the open roads to urban zones in what became known as the Second Dust Bowl. Open freeways became battlegrounds between nascent boostergangs.

A series of pandemics killed millions of victims due to widespread inability to contain them, including the Wasting Plague of 1999.

However, cybernetics technology advanced rapidly, opening up a new world of human potential. But the downsides became evident quickly - corporations and governments began to cybernetically enhance their soldiers, and as the number of people with cybernetics enhanced, they began to lose patience with the slower pace and lesser abilities of the un-enhanced. An ever-increasing ability to engage with media - a worse version of the problem of smartphones - combined with the enormous social and economic upheaval of the era led to increases in techno-shock (where you can no longer keep up with the rapid changes in technology) and what became know as [[Cyberpsychosis]].

In 2004, Megacorporations had grown large enough that the First Corporate War began between the private armies of these companies. The war lasted until 2006.

The Second Corporate War, fought between [[Petrochem]] and [[SovOil]] over oil in the South China Sea, ran from 2008 to 2010.

Also in 2008, the USA attacked the Russian weapons platform MIR XIII in space. When the Euro Space Agency intervened, the Orbital War broke out, culminating in an orbital strike on Colorado Springs in which a two-ton rock dropped from orbit devastated the city.

In 2016, the Third Corporate War is fought, but more covertly - cyber-terrorists struck at the networks of Corporations worldwide.

In 2022, the Fourth Corporate War begins between [[Arasaka]] and [[Militech International|Militech]]. Heavy combat ensues in major cities worldwide. On August 20, 2023, a nuclear device was detonated in Arasaka Towers in Night City. Atmospheric particles from the nuclear blast create a red pall in the sky worldwide. The war finally ends in 2025.

The USA became a dictatorship under President Elizabeth Kress, who declared that there would be no elections until the country was reunited.

Night City itself is reconstructed between 2030 and 2045, including the Mega Buildings - "all-in-one" arcologies designed to house enormous numbers of people.

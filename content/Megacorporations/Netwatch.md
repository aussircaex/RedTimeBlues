Technically a Non-Governmental Organization rather than a for-profit corporation, Netwatch was established as an police organization to look for illegal activity on the NET.

Since Rache Bartmoss's destruction of the Old NET, Netwatch's de facto function has transitioned. Nowadays, they act to intercept and prevent renegade netrunners who attempt to access backdoors into the Old NET, which could at best result in the runners' own deaths, and at worst, could unleash old Black ICE programs or rogue AIs onto the modern world.

Netwatch is funded by most other megacorporation in a rare moment of cooperation, as they all recognize the disruption, destruction, and threats that could arise out of the Old NET.

After the DataKrash attack that destroyed the old NET, new infrastructure was required to construct the new regional NETs that form the modern communications networks of the world. Enter Ziggurat, who updated the old fiber-optic networks, created the CitiNET protocol, and provided all other services expected of an ISP.

They also operate the Garden, the most popular content-sharing and social media hub in Night City, where anyone can set up their own Garden Patch to share videos, posts, braindances, and other content.

Originating as a wartime manufacturing company in Imperial Japan during World War II, Arasaka has continuously grown in power and influence over the decades. Their primary business has shifted over time, and as they grew in size and power, the need for increased corporate security grew, and eventually, security became their largest and most profitable sector.

On August 20, 2023, a small nuclear device was detonated in the upper floors of Arasaka Towers in Night City, destroying much of the city center and killing over 500,000 people instantly, and hundreds of thousands more in the aftermath.

Due to the destruction of a major American city, the United States nationalized Militech and banished Arasaka's military forces from the continental USA, effectively ending the Fourth Corporate War.

Officially, Arasaka has been allowed to re-establish offices in Night City, but is still barred from maintaining any armed forces under its name. Thus, Arasaka simply licenses out its troops, who wear the uniforms of their employers - but all place their loyalty to Arasaka above all.

Though nominally controlled by patriarch Saburo Arasaka, there are three factions vying for dominance within Arasaka, hoping to earn Saburo's favor and eventually be handed ownership of the company. They are the Kiji faction, led by Hanako Arasaka, the Taka faction, led by Yorinobu Arasaka, and the Hato faction, centered around elevating Michiko Sanderson (nee Arasaka).

*Petrochemical products and agribusiness. World's largest CHOOH<sup>2</sup> producer.*

At the outset of the 4th Corporate War, [[Arasaka]] and [[Militech International|Militech]] bought up almost all of Petrochem and [[SovOil]]'s aviation fuel reserves. Both fuel companies knew this would create the appearance of a shortage, which allowed them to gouge prices. However, the need to spend massive amounts of resources to protect their oil wells and refineries left the company financially depleted.

Today, Petrochem finds itself in a similar dilemma. It is the largest producer of CHOOH<sup>2</sup> in the world and owns millions of acres of arable land to grow the wheat used to produce that fuel, but the costs to protect those assets is enormous, leading it to maintain armed forces rivaling a small country.

The most obvious way Petrochem could keep themselves in the black would be to acquire [[Biotechnica]] and not have to pay the license fees to produce CHOOH<sup>2</sup>, leading to increased speculation that conflict between the two corporations is inevitable.

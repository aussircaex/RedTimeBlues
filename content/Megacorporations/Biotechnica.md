With fossil fuels largely exhausted, Biotechnica was a small firm that exploded in size when it came up with an alternative fuel: their product : CHOOH<sup>2</sup>, pronounced "chew two" (and is not its chemical formula), a complex grain alcohol created with proprietary strains of yeast and wheat owned by the company.

While this invention made Biotechnica extremely wealthy, it lacked the facilities to manufacture CHOOH<sup>2</sup> at scale, and so it licensed out its manufacturing to other energy companies, meaning it is, in itself, small as megacorporations go, with under fifty thousand employees.

Biotechnica is active in research to bio-engineer "replacement species" for the various plants and animals that have gone extinct due to climate change.

Founded as early as 2004 as a major supplier of prosthetic limbs to the Veterans Administration of the United States, Rocklin Prosthetics hit hard financial times as prosthetic device technology advanced into full cybernetic enhancements, and Rocklin's sturdy but basic products slowly lost market share.

In 2030, the company radically shifted focus, rebranding as Rocklin Augmentics. While cybernetic enhancements had once focused on maintaining the aesthetics of human flesh and shape, Rocklin's new product line focused on artificiality and uniqueness, dropping all pretense of organic simulation. Open frameworks, bizarre colors, and inhuman proportions were their style, and the public immediately and enthusiastically adopted them.

Rocklin offers "Signature Cybernetics Series", in which popular artists design design expressive pieces so that consumers can become living expressions of their art. Rocklin enjoys a high level of popularity in Night City.

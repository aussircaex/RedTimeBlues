*"Militech, uh, tech is the best out there. Not that you should try to get your hands on any, choom - those guns ain't just for show. But if you happen to find a merc flatlined in the alley with that preem chrome, it's worth every penny ya pay me to install it - "Doc" Tor Bjornson, ripperdoc*

One of the major players in the 4th Corporate War, Militech fought [[Arasaka]] Corp until the conflict came to a sudden end when the government of the United States directly intervened and nationalized the company.

While their wartime losses and the seizure of the corporation by the government have weakened it, Militech has been covertly attempting to rebuild its strength. It is still the largest producer and seller of military weapons, and the US military is its largest customer. However, it still operates as a mercenary company, and so its soldiers can be found working for anyone with enough money to hire them, and their presence tends to signify assets worth protecting.

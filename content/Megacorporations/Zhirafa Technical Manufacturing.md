*Manufacturer of drones, autonomous robotics, and construction mecha*

*"See, I like drones. With drones, you know exactly how they're spying on you and what they can see. And they're so satisfying to smash." - King Gorilla, Animals ganger*

In the wake of the 4th Corporate War, Zhirafa invented the GRAF3 construction robot, which was a cheap and effective means of cleaning up the massive destruction wrought by the war. Their enormous success has led to Zhirafa becoming the world's premier developer of robots, drones, and mechas. As a Neo-Soviet company, their presence in Night City is small, but basically everyone knows that drones and robots will play an enormous role in the next corporate conflicts.

*Ambulance and paramedic services*

*"By now, I don't even see the bodies as people. I see them as either parts or pickups" - Trauma Team employee*

Equipped with top-of-the-line AV type vehicles, the world's finest paramedics, and state-of-the-art medical technology and training, Trauma Team is *the* company to rely on to provide you emergency medical services. If you're a client, of course.

The company maintained neutrality during the 4th Corporate War, but the general increased violence of the period meant that Trauma Team's paramedics now come equipped with military surplus hardware - armor, weapons, and even power armor support.

Edgerunners have been known to buy a single coverage chip from Trauma Team to use if one of their number is grievously wounded, with the hopes that they'll get some incidental treatment for the rest of the team once they arrive.

<i>"I'll say one thing for Continental Brands - they know how to cook a microwavable steak-flavored synth burrito." - Victim 2, "So You Want to Rob a Convenience Store" XBD, circa 2042</i>

Continental Brands originated as a subsidiary of [[Petrochem]]'s American New Products Division with a simple mission: find ways to sell Americans more food than they bought last year.

The impossibility of sustaining infinite growth in the market eventually led to an internal revolt in which the wealthy oligarchs within the company consolidated their private ownership and suddenly cut out the middleman, Petrochem in 2040.

For what would seem to be an innocuous foodstuffs company, Continental Brands maintains a particularly vicious and cutthroat corporate culture.

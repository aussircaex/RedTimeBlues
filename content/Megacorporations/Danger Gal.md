Founded by the then-20-year-old Michiko Arasaka, Danger Gal is essentially a private detective agency centered entirely around Michiko, who has become a major celebrity due to being involved in a number of high-profile cases.

Hiring Danger Gal, and thus hiring Michiko, is unaffordable to all but the wealthiest clients. Frequently, her cases uncover or dismantle covert Arasaka operations in Night City.

In other words, Edgerunners who run into Danger Gal while on a job would be wise to flee immediately - you're not the main character of the story, you're the nameless punk about to get flatlined by the heroes.

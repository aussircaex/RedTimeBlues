A breakaway from the Neo-Soviet Union's state-run industry, the Neo-Soviet Oil Combine, or SovOil, controls some of the world's largest remaining oil reserves. Fully aware of the limited nature of their assets, they came into conflict with [[Petrochem]] in the 2010s in a short but savage war that grievously wounded both companies. While Petrochem moved towards CHOOH<sup>2</sup> as its future, SovOil was forced to refocus on its untapped oil fields in Siberia. Both corporations know that another clash is inevitable.

The 4th Corporate War delayed that conflict, as both corps profited from sales to [[Arasaka]] and [[Militech International|Militech]] but were forced to spend much of that profit securing their existing assets. Both companies are in the process of playing the long game, setting up pieces for what promises to be a devastating showdown.

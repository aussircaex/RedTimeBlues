The "Time of the Red" refers to two things.

First, the immediate two-year period after the nuking of Arasaka Towers in 2023 where the sky over Night City was turned red due the the particles thrown into the sky from the blast.

Second, it refers to the two-decade-long period of recovery after the Fourth Corporate War, leading right to the modern day - 2045. Even today, the sky at dawn and dusk glows a lurid red color, and occasional "blood rain" falls, creating a red, muddle sludge all over the city.

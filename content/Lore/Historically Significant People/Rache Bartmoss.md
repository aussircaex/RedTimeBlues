Legendary hacker and netrunner. Bartmoss was one of the world's best runners by the age of eighteen, becoming friends with such names as Spider Murphy and Alt Cunningham.

While he considered himself an anti-corporate rebel, Bartmoss had his unethical side - as a pet project, he arranged for everyone in his home building to have neural implants, secretly installed hidden subconscious personality programs into them, and thus controlled them to use for security and surveillance.

As became known later, his physical body died in 2020, but his life-support machines kept him cryogenically cooled and alive in the Net for two more years. Finally, his body was tracked down and killed by corporate mercenaries, but not before he had uploaded his magnum opus: the RABIDS viruses that caused the DataKrash.
